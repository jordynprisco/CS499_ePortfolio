Professional Self-Assessment

CS 499 ePortfolio

Completing the Computer Science program and developing this ePortfolio has helped me see my growth as both a technical professional and a problem solver. When I began the program, I was focused primarily on learning syntax and core programming concepts. Over time, my perspective shifted toward designing complete systems, thinking critically about efficiency and security, and understanding how software supports real organizational needs.

Throughout the program, I strengthened my foundation in programming, object oriented design, data structures, algorithms, databases, and security. In courses focused on software development, I built applications in Java and C++, emphasizing modular structure, testing, and maintainability. In data structures and algorithms, I learned to evaluate time and space complexity and select appropriate structures such as arrays, linked lists, trees, and hash maps based on the problem. This changed how I approach development. I now think beyond functionality and consider scalability and performance from the beginning.

Although my coursework did not include formal team based assignments, collaboration and communication have been central to my professional development outside of school. Working as a healthcare data analyst has required clear communication with technical and nontechnical stakeholders. I regularly translate technical issues into language that leadership and other teams can understand. That experience strengthened my ability to document processes, explain system behavior, and align technical solutions with organizational goals.

My coursework also helped strengthen my understanding of how systems work together. I’ve implemented CRUD functionality, designed relational schemas, and integrated databases with applications. I also gained practice with security principles such as hashing passwords and evaluating vulnerabilities. These experiences reinforced the importance of building systems that are not only functional but also secure and maintainable.

Security has been another major area of growth. I performed vulnerability assessments, reviewed dependency reports, and analyzed risks in web applications. I gained experience with hashing, secure communication using certificates, and understanding how authentication and access control protect systems. These experiences shaped my habit of thinking about security early in the design process rather than treating it as an afterthought.

The updates of the artifact in this ePortfolio represent different dimensions of my development. Some highlight core programming and algorithmic thinking while others demonstrate database integration, system design, and security principles. Together, they present a cohesive picture of my ability to design, develop, and evaluate secure and efficient software systems.

Overall, this program has strengthened my technical confidence and clarified my professional direction. I now approach problems with a structured, security minded, and efficiency focused mindset. This portfolio demonstrates that I am prepared to contribute as a capable and thoughtful computer science professional.
